Machine learning insights for identifying churn risk and improving customer retention.

Understanding Customer Churn

Customer Churn occurs when a customer stops using a service, cancels a subscription, or no longer makes purchases. Churn prediction helps businesses identify customers who may be at risk before they leave.

🤖

Machine Learning Risk Prediction

Machine learning models analyze customer attributes and historical behavior to identify patterns associated with churn. The system generates a churn probability and classifies customers according to their level of risk.

🎁

Data-Driven Retention

Once high-risk customers are identified, businesses can use these insights to prioritize retention efforts, improve customer experience, and develop targeted strategies to reduce customer loss.

How the XGBoost ML Prediction Pipeline Works

Data Signals
